# Second Brain

> [!summary] In one line
> An external, searchable store of what you have read and thought, so you can
> retrieve and build on it instead of re-deriving it.

## What it is

A personal knowledge system built on capture → link → retrieve. Most note apps
fail at the *link* step, so notes become a graveyard. This vault automates
linking: sources are broken into atomic pages and cross-referenced, so retrieval
actually works months later.

## Why it matters

- You ask your own vault before searching the web for things you already know.
- Knowledge is connected, not siloed in isolated notes.
- It stays plain Markdown you own.
