---
description: Run a structured reasoning loop before a hard decision.
argument-hint: <question or decision>
---

Reason carefully about: $ARGUMENTS

Work through these stages explicitly rather than giving a snap answer:

1. **Frame** — restate the decision and what "good" looks like.
2. **Context** — pull relevant facts from the `wiki/` vault (read `hot.md`, then
   `index.md`, then specific pages). Cite what you use.
3. **Options** — lay out the real alternatives.
4. **Trade-offs** — for each option: upside, downside, what must be true.
5. **Risks & unknowns** — what could make this wrong; what you'd need to learn.
6. **Second-order effects** — what each choice makes easier/harder later.
7. **Recommendation** — pick one, state your confidence and the key assumption.

Offer to `/save` the reasoning as a decision note in `wiki/notes/`.
