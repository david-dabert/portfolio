---
description: Check, scaffold, or repair the Markdown knowledge vault.
---

Read `WIKI.md` at the repo root, then operate on the `wiki/` vault.

1. Verify the vault structure exists (`index.md`, `hot.md`, `.raw/`,
   `templates/`, `concepts/`, `people/`, `sources/`, `notes/`). Create anything
   missing from the templates.
2. If the vault is brand new, ask the user exactly one question: **"What is this
   vault for?"** and record the answer at the top of `wiki/index.md`.
3. Report what exists, what was created/repaired, and the current page count.

Do not install plugins, clone repos, or add MCP servers. Content only.
